Training prerequisite (as reported; verify against the GR)

  • Senior Grade (12 yrs): 10 days of training, completed in 2 of the 3 years before completing 12 years.
  • Selection Grade (24 yrs): 40 days of training, 10 days a year, completed in 4 of the 5 years before completing 24 years.
  • Training was made a condition by the GR of 20.07.2021 (No. शिप्रधो २०१९/प्र.क्र.४३/प्रशिक्षण). SCERT 2026 offline training at DIETs: 13–22 Nov 2026 (registration closed 20.09.2026; fee ₹2,000).

8th Pay Commission: Status & Customisable Projection

The Commission has not announced a fitment factor or pay matrix. Everything in the projection box below is your own assumption, not an official outcome.

What is on record

  • Constitution: Ministry of Finance (Dept. of Expenditure) notified the Terms of Reference on 03.11.2025; Union Cabinet approved the ToR on 28.10.2025.
  • Members: Justice Ranjana Prakash Desai (Chairperson), Prof. Pulak Ghosh (Part-time Member), Pankaj Jain (Member-Secretary).
  • Timeline: Report within 18 months of constitution (about May 2027 if counted from 03.11.2025); interim reports are permitted.
  • Scope: Central Government employees, All India Services, Defence, UTs. State employees are not covered directly — states adopt revisions on their own. Maharashtra adopted the 7th CPC through the Maharashtra Civil Services (Revised Pay) Rules, 2019, effective 01.01.2016 (fixation factor 2.57).
  • Gratuity: ToR asks the Commission to review death-cum-retirement gratuity for NPS (including UPS) employees.
  • Not yet decided: fitment factor, pay matrix, pension formula. 2.57 (7th CPC, 2016) is the last official factor. Reference date reported as 01.01.2026; roll-out date not fixed.
  • Maharashtra: No announced Maharashtra pay commission for the 8th cycle was found in the sources checked.

Assumptions & Disclaimer

  • Contributions: 10% (employee) + 14% (State) of Basic + DA, compounded annually with mid-year timing. DA is held at the current rate unless the 8th CPC scenario is applied.
  • Final basic pay = current basic × (1 + increment)(years - 1). Central UPS uses the average of the last two pay levels as the 12-month average.
  • Gratuity = ¼ × (Basic + DA) × completed six-monthly periods, limited to 33 periods (16.5 months' emoluments) and the editable ceiling.
  • Senior/Selection Grade is modelled as a % uplift in basic at the 12th and 24th service year (your assumption). Total service for pension and gratuity = service completed so far + projection years.
  • Pure NPS annuity = 40% of corpus × annuity rate ÷ 12 (life annuity, no return of purchase price, not indexed).
  • Revised NPS: 50% of last basic for 20+ years; proportionate (modelled as years ÷ 20 × 50%) for 10–20 years; minimum ₹7,500; plus DR. 60% of the corpus goes to the State; the annuity from the other 40% is adjusted against the State-payable pension. Central UPS: 50% of 12-month average basic at 25 years (proportionate below), ₹10,000 minimum at 10 years, lump sum of 10% of monthly emoluments per six months, plus DR.
  • Illustrative planning tool, not official advice. Verify against the original Government Resolutions and consult your DDO / Pension Cell before opting.
